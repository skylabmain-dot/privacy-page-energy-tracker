Privacy Policy

App: Helio. Energy Tracker
Last updated: March 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Helio. Energy Tracker ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide the app's functionality:

  • Google Account (email address) — only if you choose to sign in with Google. This is used solely to sync your habits across your devices. We do not store your password.
  • Apple ID (email address) — only if you choose to sign in with Apple. This is used solely to sync your habits across your devices.
  • Habit data you create — your habits, energy values, and streaks are stored in Firebase (Google) linked to your account.
  • Crash reports — we use Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash diagnostics. This data helps us fix bugs and improve app stability. It cannot identify you personally.
  • Anonymous usage analytics — we use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (e.g., which screens are visited). This data is aggregated and cannot identify you personally.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, or physical address.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell your data to any third party.
  • We do not send marketing emails.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Sync your habits and progress across your devices when signed in.
  • Diagnose and fix crashes to improve app stability.
  • Improve the app based on anonymous, aggregated usage patterns.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In — Google Privacy Policy
  • Sign in with Apple — Apple Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics) — Firebase Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage and Security

Your habit data is stored securely in Google Firebase. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your data at any time.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account), or by contacting us directly.

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
